Camping

Camping is available for the full weekend near The Bellbrook Hotel, so you don't have to miss a minute of The Yowie Festival. Entry to the festival itself is free. There are two camping options:

Bellmeadow Homestead

Camping is hosted by Bellmeadow Homestead, a heritage homestead on 44 acres of farmland in Bellbrook, near the Macleay River.

Camping bookings go through Bellmeadow Homestead directly — phone +61 (0) 409 124 438 or email awerservices@gmail.com. Primitive camping is $10 per site (caravan/tent) per night, or $5 per swag per night — no amenities, water or power provided, so please bring what you need and take your rubbish with you. A flushable camping toilet inside a privacy tent is available on request (refundable deposit and daily charge apply). The Bellbrook General Store & Post Office, with a cafe and everyday essentials, is in town on Main Street.

Bellbrook Cabins
