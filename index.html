About the weekend

The Yowie Festival is a three-day community fundraiser in Bellbrook, NSW, raising funds for Macleay Valley Hospice and the Bellbrook community. From 2–4 October 2026, expect live music, markets, camping, a wood chopping competition, a fishing competition, whip cracking displays, face painting and plenty of Big Foot fun for the whole family.
