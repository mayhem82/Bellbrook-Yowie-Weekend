🦶 The Yowie FestivalMap Planner (draft tool)

Map planner

Real OpenStreetMap tiles, your real activity icons. Tap a button to drop a pin of that type, drag it to where it really belongs. Your arrangement saves itself in this browser automatically, so it's still here next time you open the page. When you're happy with it, tap Export for Claude and paste the result straight into the chat — exact coordinates, no screenshots needed.

Why export instead of Claude just looking at the map? Claude can't open this live page or watch it update — a screenshot only lets it guess where a pin sits in a grid of pixels, which is never accurate enough for real placement. Exporting turns your pins into exact GPS coordinates as plain text, which you paste back into the chat so Claude can put them on the real festival map precisely, not approximately.
