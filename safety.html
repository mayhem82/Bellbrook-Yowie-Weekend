Safety at The Yowie Festival

The Yowie Festival is a family event, and everyone's safety is the top priority. The festival is an outdoor community event, and conditions can change quickly, particularly with weather, fire and river conditions. Children must be supervised at all times. Please follow the directions of event staff, volunteers and marshals at all times, and look out for each other over the weekend.

Before and during the festival, keep up to date with weather warnings and forecasts, and follow any directions issued by festival organisers or emergency services. If severe weather is forecast, please follow instructions from festival staff and move to a safe location when directed.

Phone service in the area can be intermittent — Telstra appears to be the most reliable carrier.

In an emergency

Life-threatening emergency

Call 000

For police, fire or ambulance, call 000 immediately and tell the operator you're at The Bellbrook Hotel, Bellbrook.

First aid

Slim Dusty Room

Approach a member of the Security Team, Festival Volunteers or Bellbrook Hotel staff, who can assist you and direct you to first aid support in the Slim Dusty Room.

Defibrillator

AED location

A defibrillator (AED) is on the front wall of The Bellbrook Hotel, at the General Store end (left-hand side when facing the hotel). Familiarise yourself with its location when you arrive. In an emergency, alert Security, a Festival Volunteer or Hotel staff member immediately.

Good to know

Sun & heat

Slip, slop, slap

Early October can still be warm. Bring a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle, and take breaks in the shade.

Weather

Rain or shine

The festival runs rain or shine unless conditions become unsafe. Check this site or the festival's Facebook page for any updates.

Alcohol

Responsible service

Any licensed bar on-site is subject to responsible service of alcohol (RSA). Photo ID may be required.

Fire safety

Camping & campfires

If you're camping, follow all festival fire safety requirements and any directions from the NSW Rural Fire Service. Never leave a campfire unattended, and make sure fires are completely extinguished before leaving them.

Parking & traffic

Follow the marshals

Please follow the directions of parking marshals and drive slowly and carefully around pedestrians on-site.

Access

Accessibility

If you have accessibility needs, contact us ahead of the event so we can help plan your visit.

Beat the heat

October in the Upper Macleay can bring warm and sunny conditions. If the weather is hot, please take care of yourself and your family:

  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Carry a refillable water bottle.
  • Stay in the shade whenever possible.
  • Wear loose-fitting, lightweight clothing.
  • Wear a hat and use sunscreen.
  • Take regular breaks from the sun.
  • Avoid excessive alcohol consumption in hot weather.
  • Check on children and anyone who may be more vulnerable to the heat.
  • Don't wait until you feel thirsty to drink water.

If you start feeling unwell from the heat, move to a cool or shaded area, drink water and seek assistance from Festival Volunteers, Security or Bellbrook Hotel staff.

Camping & fire safety

If you're camping at The Yowie Festival, please follow all festival fire safety requirements and any directions issued by the NSW Rural Fire Service.

  • Never leave a campfire unattended.
  • Make sure fires are completely extinguished before leaving them.
  • During periods of high or extreme fire danger, additional restrictions may apply.
  • Please follow all instructions from festival organisers and emergency services.
