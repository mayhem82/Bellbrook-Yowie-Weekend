Safety at The Yowie Festival

The Yowie Festival is a family event, and everyone's safety is the top priority. Please follow the directions of event staff, volunteers and marshals at all times, and look out for each other over the weekend.

In an emergency

Life-threatening emergency

Call 000

For police, fire or ambulance, call 000 immediately and tell the operator you're at The Bellbrook Hotel, Bellbrook.

Good to know

Sun & heat

Slip, slop, slap

Early October can still be warm. Bring a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle, and take breaks in the shade.

Weather

Rain or shine

The festival runs rain or shine unless conditions become unsafe. Check this site or the festival's Facebook page for any updates.

Alcohol

Responsible service

Any licensed bar on-site is subject to responsible service of alcohol (RSA). Photo ID may be required.

Fire safety

Camping & campfires

Follow any total fire ban notices and only light fires in designated, permitted areas. Check current NSW fire danger ratings before you travel.

Parking & traffic

Follow the marshals

Please follow the directions of parking marshals and drive slowly and carefully around pedestrians on-site.

Access

Accessibility

If you have accessibility needs, contact us ahead of the event so we can help plan your visit.
