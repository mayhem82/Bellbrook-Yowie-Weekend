Weekend program

The Yowie Festival runs across three days, Friday 2 to Sunday 4 October 2026, at The Bellbrook Hotel. Markets trade 11:00am–4:00pm Saturday and Sunday, with wood chopping and whip cracking running alongside the markets. The live music lineup for Saturday and Sunday is confirmed — see the full lineup below. Friday's lineup is still being finalised. A few other activity times will be announced closer to the event.

The entertainment lineup is still being finalised. Confirmed so far: a bucking bull, organised by The Bellbrook Hotel; a Yowie Hunt along Postman's Trail, Saturday 8–9am registration at Bellmeadow Homestead; Slim Dusty Tours, organised by Kate; and gumboot throwing. Days and times for the bucking bull, Slim Dusty Tours and gumboot throwing are still to come. Kids' activities are also being organised and will be added here once confirmed.
