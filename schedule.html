Weekend program

The Yowie Festival runs across three days, Friday 2 to Sunday 4 October 2026, at The Bellbrook Hotel — a historic heritage pub since 1913 and an attraction in its own right, freshly refurbished with a new back deck overlooking the Macleay River. The pub opens daily from 11am for food and drinks, so it's worth the visit any day of the weekend, even outside the scheduled program below. Friday's scheduled program kicks off in the evening with live music from 6:30pm — see the full lineup below. Markets trade 11:00am–4:00pm Saturday and Sunday, with wood chopping and whip cracking running alongside the markets, and the full live music running order is confirmed for all three days.

The entertainment lineup is still being finalised. Confirmed so far: a bucking bull, Sunday 1:00–4:00pm; a Yowie Hunt along Postman's Trail, Saturday 8–9am registration at Bellmeadow Homestead; and Slim Dusty Tours, organised by Kate (day/time still to come). Gumboot throwing is also confirmed as happening, day/time still to come. Kids' activities are also being organised and will be added here once confirmed.
