Weekend program

The Yowie Festival runs across three days, Friday 2 to Sunday 4 October 2026, at The Bellbrook Hotel. Markets trade 11:00am–4:00pm Saturday and Sunday, with wood chopping and whip cracking running alongside the markets. The live music lineup for Saturday and Sunday is confirmed — see the full lineup below. Friday's lineup is still a draft. Other activity times haven't been published yet, so the day-by-day grouping below is a best guess — update it once the official running order is out.

The entertainment lineup is still being finalised. Confirmed so far: a bucking bull, organised by The Bellbrook Hotel; a Yowie Hunt along Postman's Trail, back again this year — a car-drive activity taking approximately 2 hours; Slim Dusty Tours, organised by Kate; and gumboot throwing. Days and times for all four are still to come. Kids' activities are also being organised and will be added here once confirmed.
