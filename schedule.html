Weekend program

The Yowie Festival runs across three days, Friday 2 to Sunday 4 October 2026, at The Bellbrook Hotel — a historic heritage pub since 1913 and an attraction in its own right, freshly refurbished with a new back deck overlooking the Macleay River. The pub opens daily from 11am for food and drinks, so it's worth the visit any day of the weekend, even outside the scheduled program below. Each day's table below is the complete running order for that day — activities and live music together, in time order, so there's just one list to check.

Parking on-site is monitored by attendants.
