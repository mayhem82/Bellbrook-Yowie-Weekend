Off-road adventure

Yowie Hunt

Can you find a Yowie? Tag your crew, fuel up, and get ready for an off-road adventure like no other, back again this year along Postman's Trail — a car-drive activity taking approximately 2 hours. Bring your mates, and your courage. The bush is waiting… but so is he.

Registrations are Saturday 3 October, 8:00–9:00am only, meeting at Bellmeadow Homestead.

$10 per car entry includes a special map to guide you through the tracks. You'll need a 4x4 or other capable off-road vehicle, a sense of adventure, and a sharp eye for mystery — he could be lurking anywhere!
